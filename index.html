Rooms




Infants

Safe Environment, Individual Care, Sign language,Feeding and Naps, exploration of natural and everyday objects, and time for outdoor discovery.



Toddlers

Provide them with more child friendly space, activities, materials and food to explore with their senses and whole body.



Preschool

Explore and learn through a carefully designed methodology of the HighScope Curriculum called “active participatory learning”.



Pre-K

Developed and prepared for everything they are going to encounter in the school,eating on their own, making friends, reading, writing etc.

Our Approach


We are Little Rainbow Daycare & Nursery, a proud community of 51798 parents across more than 190 centers in 26 cities.

We are dedicated to holistic child development through the US-based HighScope Curriculum, which promotes social, emotional, and cognitive growth.
