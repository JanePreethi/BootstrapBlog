About us

Your Child Is in Great Hands


Philosophy


From a lonely parenting journey, with no handbook on the Whats and the Hows to building a community to help sail through parenting, nutrition, learning and more…


Little Rainbow is leading chain of Preschools and Daycare centres, valued for supporting over 60,000 families through top-notch learning and care for their children. Our focus is on creating a safe, nurturing and engaging environment for little ones to learn and grow, supported by passionate facilitators, modern infrastructure, and a proven pedagogy. Little Rainbow preschool learning program is managed by GRACEL Trust.

Health & Safety


At Little Rainbow, we go the extra mile, surpassing the conventional daycare and preschool experience. Our passionate team, state-of-the-art facilities, stringent health and safety protocols and thoughtfully designed learning approach combine to provide a safe, secure and enriching place for your child.

Join us at Little Rainbow, where together we'll nurture your little one's holistic growth and development.
